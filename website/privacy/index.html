Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Transparent, privacy-first design: no accounts, no tracking, and local storage.

Last Updated: October 2026

Estonia Driving Theory is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. We believe your learning journey is your personal business. The app does not require a user account, does not use external tracking or analytics, and keeps your learning data safely on your own device.

1. Local Data Storage

All study progress, answered questions, mistake records, daily goals, readiness indicators, and user settings are stored locally on your device (using iOS native storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers or third parties.

2. Purchases & StoreKit

In-app purchases (Monthly Premium and Lifetime Premium) are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card, debit card, or payment billing information. All financial transactions are governed by Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions.

3. Local Notifications

If enabled by you, local notifications may be scheduled on your device for optional study reminders and goal nudges. These notifications are generated locally by iOS without any external notification servers. You can enable, disable, or customize notifications at any time through iOS Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

The application relies only on standard Apple iOS platform services necessary for App Store distribution, receipt validation, and StoreKit purchases. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or analytics providers.

5. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or usage activity under any circumstances.
