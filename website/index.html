Visual Catalogue

Learn Estonia’s Road Signs

Master Estonia's traffic signs with an intuitive visual library, regulatory explanations, and interactive memory tools.

Sign 211

Peatee (Priority Road)

Grants right of way at un-signaled crossroads along this road until sign 212.

Sign 221

Anna teed (Give Way)

Driver must yield right of way to vehicles approaching on the intersecting road.

Sign 222

Peatu ja anna teed (Stop)

Mandatory full stop before the stop line and give way to all intersecting traffic.

Sign 311

Suurim kiirus (Speed Limit 50)

Prohibits driving at a speed greater than 50 km/h in the indicated zone.

Sign 331

Sissesõidu keeld (No Entry)

Entry prohibited for all vehicles. Commonly used at one-way street exits.

Sign 421

Ringliiklus (Roundabout)

Traffic flows in the direction of arrows counter-clockwise around the island.

Sign 543

Ülekäigurada (Crossing)

Marks an unsignaled pedestrian crossing where drivers must yield to pedestrians.
